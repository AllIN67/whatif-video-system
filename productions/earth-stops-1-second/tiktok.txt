=== FACT SHEET (for comment replies) ===
- Equator rotation speed: ~1,040 mph (24,901 mi / 23.93 h sidereal day)
- New York (40.7° N): 1,040 × cos(40.7°) ≈ 790 mph
- In 1 second at 790 mph you would travel ~1,155 ft (352 m)
- Speed of sound at sea level (15 °C): ~761 mph → 790 mph winds are supersonic
- Fastest recorded tropical-cyclone gust: 253 mph (Cyclone Olivia, Barrow Island, 1996, WMO)
- At the North Pole your rotation speed is ~0 mph
